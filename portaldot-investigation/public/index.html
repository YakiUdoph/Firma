 The method

        
A narrow claim,
proved carefully.

        
FIRMA is designed around one question. It does not turn missing observations into reassuring answers.

      

      
    
        
  1. 01
    Lock the moment

  2. 02

Scope and safety

        
Verification, not custody.

      

      

        
FIRMA's verifier reads public network state. Its separate optional testnet anchoring flow may ask SubWallet to sign one exact zero-value commitment transaction only after an explicit review and confirmation. FIRMA never handles private keys or recovery phrases.

        

          Technical scope
          
The verifier fingerprints the runtime, reads Assets and Revive storage, maps the EVM address to its native account, and performs ERC-20-compatible eth_call reads at the same finalized height. Exact base-unit values remain arbitrary-precision integers. Results describe only the listed observations at that block and are not an audit of write behavior.
