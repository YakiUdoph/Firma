        See the technical method
        
FIRMA fingerprints the live runtime, reads Assets and Revive storage, maps the EVM account to its native account, and calls the ERC-20 surface with eth_call at the same finalized height. Exact base-unit values stay as arbitrary-precision integers. The evidence retains the nine technical PASS, FAIL, or UNKNOWN classifications.
